What we store on our servers

  • Account: your email, display name and a hashed password (or the name, email and picture a sign-in provider such as Google, GitHub, Facebook or X gives us).
  • Profile: your profile picture and short bio, if you add them.
  • Chat: messages, pictures, notes/lorebook/video previews you share, reactions and replies in the global chat, group chats and private chats. Each chat keeps only the most recent messages (about 120).
  • Social: your friend code, friends, friend requests, groups you are in, and people you have blocked.
  • Reports: if you report someone, or someone reports you, we keep the report, the reported message text and who sent it.

What stays on your device

Your notes, lorebooks, videos, AI chat history and settings are stored in your own browser. We do not receive them unless you share them in a chat or choose them as context for the AI. If you clear your browser data they are gone, so use the backup button.

Other companies that handle data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores the data above.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model providers it uses receive the messages you send to the AI page (and any notes you attach), and the text of chat messages and pictures so they can be checked automatically for illegal content.
  • Voice calls go directly between participants. If a relay server is needed, the audio passes through it but is not recorded by us.

Cookies and storage

We use one login cookie and your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can edit your name, picture and bio at any time, block and report people, and delete your account from the profile menu ("Delete my account"). Deleting removes your account, picture, bio, friend links and groups you own. Messages you already sent in chats may stay visible to other people for a while, because chats keep a short history. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. If you live in the EU/EEA or UK you have rights under data protection law, including access, correction, erasure, objection and complaint to your data protection authority.
